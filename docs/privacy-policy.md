# Privacy Policy for Laundry Loyalty

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Laundry Loyalty stores customer and shop visit information locally on the Android device where the app is installed.

## Information Stored

The app may store the following information for laundry shop operations:

- Customer name
- Mobile number
- Address
- Signature image
- Visit dates
- Laundry load counts
- Payment amount and payment date
- Free-load redemption status

## How Information Is Used

This information is used only for laundry shop customer tracking, payment reporting, and loyalty rewards.

## Data Sharing and Transmission

This release does not send customer data to external servers. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cloud sync, account login, or remote backup features.

The app's data remains on the device unless the shop exports records or backs up/transfers the device data outside the app. Shop owners are responsible for handling exported customer records securely.

## Data Deletion

Local records can be deleted by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app from the Android device. A built-in customer record deletion feature may be added in a future release.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details listed on the Google Play store listing.
